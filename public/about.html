About BVCITS

Over 25 years of engineering leadership, research innovation, and community development in the Godavari delta region.

Delta Engineering Ethos

Where the Godavari meets engineering.

Bonam Venkata Chalamayya Institute of Technology & Science (BVCITS) was established at Batlapalem, Amalapuram, to impart high-standard engineering and management education to students from across coastal Andhra Pradesh.


With autonomous curriculum approval from UGC and JNTUK, BVCITS integrates cutting-edge academic modules, full-stack coding laboratories, industry internships, and extensive Campus Recruitment Training (CRT).


🏛 Vision

To be a premier center of technical excellence, producing globally competent, innovative, and ethically grounded engineers and managers committed to regional and national growth.

🎯 Mission

Deliver industry-aligned outcome-based technical education; foster entrepreneurship and R&D; empower students through soft skills and practical lab training; serve the Godavari delta community with sustainable engineering solutions.

⭐ Quality Policy

BVCITS is committed to continuous enhancement in teaching-learning standards, research publications, student placements, and infrastructure development to exceed global standards.
