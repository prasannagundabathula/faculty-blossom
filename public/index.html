Institutional Overview

Engineering minds for a developing delta.

BVCITS sits in Batlapalem, Amalapuram, in the agricultural and aquacultural heartland of East Godavari district. The institute was established with a clear mission: technical education should be rigorous enough for national placement drives, and grounded enough to matter locally — in irrigation technology, industrial automation, and regional entrepreneurship.

🏛 Vision

To be a premier center of technical excellence, producing globally competent, innovative, and ethically grounded engineers and managers committed to regional and national growth.

🎯 Mission

Deliver industry-aligned outcome-based technical education; foster entrepreneurship and R&D; empower students through soft skills and practical lab training; serve the Godavari delta community with sustainable engineering solutions.
